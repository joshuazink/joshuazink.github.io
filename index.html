Joshua Zink, D.M.A.

Assistant Professor & Program Director, Kennesaw State University · Kennesaw, GA / Atlanta Metro

I'm a learning designer and OD practitioner whose work spans higher education, workplace learning, and organizational development. I hold a D.M.A. from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln and serve as Assistant Professor and director of the Opera program at Kennesaw State University, one of the university's major performing arts programs.

My design philosophy: start with the performance problem, not the content. Every course here was built around a real workplace gap -- what people need to do differently, not just what they should know. I approach instructional design the same way I approach coaching: figure out what's actually in the way, then build something that removes it.

Two things shape how I think about high-stakes work. The first is my background as a professional opera artist: live, in front of an audience, one take. No editing after the fact. The second is endurance sport. Thirteen years of competitive running across every distance from the 5K to the marathon, and now active road cycling and racing. Both teach the same lesson: discipline and genuine preparation are the only things that hold up under pressure. That mindset carries directly into how I design, facilitate, and coach.
