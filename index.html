Instructional Design Portfolio — 4 Courses

Adult Learning Science  ·  L&D Practitioners

Designing Effective Workplace Learning: The Science of Adult Training

A six-lesson course translating adult learning research into practical instructional design decisions. Covers Knowles' andragogy, cognitive load theory, the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve, Kirkpatrick's evaluation framework, and Mayer's multimedia learning principles — with applied examples throughout. Written for L&D professionals and managers who design or commission training.
